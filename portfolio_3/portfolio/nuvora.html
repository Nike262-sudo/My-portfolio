01 — Project Overview

A digital presence built around clarity.

Nuvora Studios needed a website that felt as considered as the digital products they create. The goal was to combine strong typography, clear structure, and immersive visuals without making the experience feel complicated.

The final direction uses an editorial layout, generous whitespace, strong imagery, and subtle motion to give the studio a confident digital presence.

02 — Design Direction

Quiet structure. Strong visual language.

The visual direction was built around oversized typography, restrained colour, generous spacing, and imagery that gives the work room to breathe.

03 — Interaction & Experience

Designed to move with the user.

Motion was used to make the experience feel alive without taking attention away from the work. Scroll transitions, image reveals, and interactive sections create a more considered journey through the site.

04 — Final Design

A digital home for the studio's work.

The final experience brings the visual system and interactions together into a focused, editorial website for Nuvora Studios.
