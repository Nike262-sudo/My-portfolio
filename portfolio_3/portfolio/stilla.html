Case Study · Furniture Web Experience

STILLA

A calm, editorial furniture experience shaped by restraint.

STILLA is a conceptual furniture website inspired by Scandinavian interiors and print catalogues. The work focused on creating a premium atmosphere without relying on visual noise.

Role

Product Designer · Frontend Developer

Tools

Figma · React · Framer Motion

Timeline

Concept exploration

View Live Site Next Project

Project Overview

Luxury through restraint, not decoration.

The objective was not to make another furniture storefront. It was to design a digital space that feels quiet, intentional, and premium from the first scroll.

Every decision was filtered through one question: does this increase clarity and calm, or does it simply add noise?

The Challenge

Most furniture websites compete through dense interfaces, promotional blocks, and aggressive motion. That pattern made premium pieces feel transactional.

STILLA needed to communicate quality through pacing, hierarchy, and material-focused presentation.

Design Goals

Set a clear system before styling screens.

  • 01Create an editorial reading rhythm with deliberate spacing.
  • 02Let typography and product photography carry the visual weight.
  • 03Build a simple path from inspiration to collection exploration.
  • 04Keep interactions subtle, with motion supporting context rather than spectacle.

Creative Direction

Scandinavian calm, editorial pacing, tactile cues.

The direction draws from interior magazines and showroom catalogues where space is treated as part of the composition. The interface stays minimal so products, materials, and typography can lead.

Structure

Alternating narrow reading blocks and wide visual moments to create rhythm.

Motion

Gentle transitions in React + Framer Motion to support hierarchy and continuity.

Tone

Calm, mature, and quiet. The copy remains concise and avoids promotional language.

Interface

Minimal controls and clear navigation to keep attention on furniture and craft.

Typography

Editorial contrast with restrained utility text.

The type system pairs expressive serif headlines with neutral sans-serif body copy. Headings set the emotional tone, while supporting text stays understated and highly legible.

A home shaped by quiet details.

EB Garamond provides a timeless editorial voice, while Geist ensures clarity and consistency across navigation, product details, and supporting interface elements.

Colour & Material Palette

A restrained palette grounded in natural material references.

Neutral tones were selected to echo oak, linen, stone, and matte metal finishes. Colour supports atmosphere and readability rather than acting as decoration.

Materials

Visual direction emphasizes wood grain, fabric texture, and clean metal accents to imply quality.

Contrast

Soft contrast keeps the interface calm while preserving clear hierarchy and accessibility.

Consistency

The same palette logic carries across desktop and mobile to maintain a coherent brand tone.

Homepage Design

A composed first impression with clear hierarchy.

The homepage introduces STILLA through a restrained hero, measured copy length, and immediate pathways into featured collections. Spacing and type scale do most of the heavy lifting.

Collection Experience

Browsing collections feels curated, not crowded.

Collections are grouped with clear categories and breathing room between product stories. The user can scan quickly while still feeling the craftsmanship of each line.

Craftsmanship Section

Detail stories reinforce the premium proposition.

Dedicated craft modules slow the experience down intentionally, framing construction details as part of the product value. This section balances emotional storytelling with practical information.

Materials Section

Material transparency builds trust.

Material content is structured as concise, digestible blocks so visitors understand composition and finish choices without leaving the flow of browsing.

Featured Products

Product highlights maintain clarity at every card.

Featured cards use a reduced information set: product name, material hint, and price anchor. The result feels confident and avoids visual clutter.

Mobile Experience

Mobile keeps the same calm, with tighter pacing.

On mobile, typography scales with care, vertical spacing is rebalanced, and image modules are resized to preserve the editorial feel rather than collapsing into generic stacked blocks.

Responsive Design

One system, tuned for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

The layout shifts from wide editorial compositions on desktop to balanced two-column and then single-column patterns, with adjusted spacing and hierarchy at each breakpoint.

Development

From Figma system to React implementation.

The final front-end was developed in React, with Framer Motion used for subtle reveal transitions and interaction polish. Implementation followed the same principles as the design: keep the interface focused, performant, and easy to maintain.

Key Learnings

Premium experiences rely more on proportion, pacing, and consistency than on visual effects. Restraint required more discipline, but produced a more distinctive and believable result.

Final Outcome

STILLA presents furniture as an experience, not just a catalogue.

The case study demonstrates how a restrained visual language can still feel rich, modern, and premium. The final result is a cohesive concept ready for production refinement and real photography replacement.
