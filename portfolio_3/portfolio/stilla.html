CASE STUDY · FURNITURE WEBSITE DESIGN

STILLA

A refined furniture experience built around craftsmanship, restraint, and timeless design.

STILLA is a conceptual furniture brand website inspired by Scandinavian editorial design. The goal was to create a digital experience where typography, imagery, and whitespace work together to communicate quality without relying on excessive visual effects.

ROLE Product Designer
TYPE Marketing Website
TIMELINE 2 Weeks

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Designing a digital experience where furniture speaks louder than the interface.

STILLA is a conceptual furniture website created to explore how restraint can become a visual identity. Rather than relying on oversized animations, colourful gradients or decorative interface elements, the experience focuses on timeless typography, intentional spacing and carefully curated photography.

Every layout decision was guided by one principle: remove anything that distracts from the craftsmanship. The result is a browsing experience that feels closer to reading an editorial magazine than shopping through a traditional ecommerce catalogue.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Every design decision followed four simple principles.

01

Restraint over decoration

Rather than relying on gradients, excessive motion or visual noise, the interface remains intentionally quiet, allowing the products to become the primary visual focus.

02

Whitespace creates rhythm

Generous spacing improves readability and creates the calm editorial feeling commonly found in premium furniture catalogues and printed magazines.

03

Typography leads hierarchy

Instrument Serif provides character while Geist delivers clarity, creating a clear hierarchy without depending on oversized interface elements.

04

Photography tells the story

Large immersive imagery replaces unnecessary interface decoration, communicating material quality and craftsmanship before visitors even begin reading.

HOMEPAGE EXPERIENCE

Creating a calm first impression through typography, photography and space.

The homepage was designed to feel more like an editorial publication than a traditional ecommerce website. Large imagery, restrained motion and generous whitespace establish the brand's premium character before visitors begin exploring the collection.
